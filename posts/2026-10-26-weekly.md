## 🏆 Awards

**Team of the week: JAReusalem.** An 8-1 demolition of Viva La Raza!, with 628 points to 480.

**MVP of the week: Tyrese Maxey** (JAReusalem). 115 pts, 16 reb, 19 ast, 8 stl, 4 blk and 9 threes in 4 games, shooting 24/28 from the line.

**Upset of the week: Torta Pounders** beat mr charloooooo 5-3-1. Torta opened as $8.00 outsiders, with just a 4% chance at their lowest. For mr charloooooo, it's also the **choke of the week**: they were 96% favourites at the start.

**Bad beat: Stairway to Devin** lost rebounds to Derrick White Girl Wasted 216 to 219. Three boards. Stairway still won the matchup, so it only stings a bit.

**Best acquisition: Duncan Robinson** to Popoviched for a $1 bid. In 3 games: 59 pts and 15 threes. That's 31 fantasy points a game for a dollar.

**Worst acquisition: Marvin Bagley III** to Al-Shaida for $3. 2 games, 7 pts, 7 reb.

**Worst drop: Moussa Diabate**, cut by Popoviched and picked up by Stairway to Devin. Over the last 7 days: 9.2 pts, 9.5 reb and 2.2 stl a game.

**Bench points of the week: HE IS HIM ( G.O.A.T)** left 53.9 fantasy points on the bench. The biggest single bench game was **Wendell Carter Jr.** for The Real OG: 22 pts on 8/10 shooting.
