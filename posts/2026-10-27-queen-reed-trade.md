## 🚨 The deal

**Derrick White Girl Wasted** get: Derik Queen
**Stairway to Devin** get: Paul Reed

The trade has been accepted and is in the 24-hour league review.

## The case for Stairway

Reed's been sneaky good to start the year: 11 pts, 8.3 reb and a block in 22 mins, shooting 61%. He's just filling in while Duren is out, though. When Duren's back, Reed's projection drops to about 7 pts and 4 reb in 14 mins. That's buying at the top 📉

## The case for DWGW

Queen is 70% owned for a reason. He's a 26-minute starter projected for about 12.6 pts, 7.4 reb, 3.9 ast and a steal, and he's shooting 93% from the line so far. He has a real role, and it doesn't depend on someone else being injured.

## 🤖 What the model says

Expected wins, rest of season (weeks 2-18):

- **DWGW: +0.337.** Better odds in AST, REB and PTS, slightly worse in TO.
- **Stairway: -0.341.** Fewer turnovers and a small FG% bump, but they lose ground in AST, REB and PTS.

A third of a win either way, for one bench-level swap. That's a lot.

## 🏆 Verdict

DWGW win this comfortably. They swapped a fill-in for a starter with a secure role. Stairway get a nice week or two of rebounds, then a waiver-wire big.

**Grades:** DWGW A-, Stairway C-
