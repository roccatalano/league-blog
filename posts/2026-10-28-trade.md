Two trades have been **processed** this morning, so nobody gets to complain in review. Both are 2-for-2 swaps. Numbers are expected wins change, rest of season.

## Stairway to Devin get Buzelis and George, JAReusalem get Diabate and Lillard

**Stairway to Devin** pick up Matas Buzelis and Paul George. **JAReusalem** take back Moussa Diabate and Damian Lillard.

Stairway are the clear winners at **+0.286**. They gain 3PM (+0.121), BLK (+0.083), PTS (+0.063) and STL (+0.045). They give up AST (-0.060), TO (-0.059), REB (-0.037) and FG% (-0.034).

JAReusalem land at **-0.220**. Lillard brings 21.7 points and 3.1 threes a game, but they lose 3PM (-0.117), BLK (-0.074), PTS (-0.064) and STL (-0.035). They do gain AST (+0.066), TO (+0.060), REB (+0.039) and FG% (+0.031). Diabate is a useful rebounder, but he hits zero threes a game.

**Verdict:** Stairway to Devin **B+**, JAReusalem **D+**. Trading two scorers for a scorer and a big is a rough way to lose three-pointers.

## Al-Shaida get VanVleet and Barnes, Viva La Raza! get Okongwu and Young

**Al-Shaida** receive Fred VanVleet and Scottie Barnes. **Viva La Raza!** get Onyeka Okongwu and Trae Young.

This one is much tighter. Al-Shaida come out at **+0.048**. They gain STL (+0.078), TO (+0.053), BLK (+0.046) and REB (+0.018). They lose 3PM (-0.056), AST (-0.046) and PTS (-0.043). Giving up Young's 10.8 assists hurts, but his 3.9 turnovers go with him.

Viva La Raza! finish at **-0.112**. They gain 3PM (+0.058), AST (+0.054), PTS (+0.053), FT% (+0.017) and FG% (+0.011). They lose STL (-0.081), BLK (-0.062) and TO (-0.060). Young is a one-man assist and points machine, but the steals and turnover damage outweighs it.

**Verdict:** Al-Shaida **B-**, Viva La Raza! **C**. Young gives Viva La Raza! a fun player, but the model prefers the balanced haul. 🏀

Both trades are counted over weeks 2-18, and all six players are healthy right now.
