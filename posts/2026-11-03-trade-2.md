## Breaking: another 2-for-2 in league review

Hot on the heels of the earlier blockbuster, **JAReusalem** and **Viva La Raza!** have agreed a swap of their own. It's accepted but in league review, so no players have moved yet.

- **JAReusalem gets:** Aaron Gordon and Rui Hachimura
- **Viva La Raza! gets:** Kyle Kuzma and Moussa Diabate

## The case for JAReusalem

This is a scoring and threes play. Gordon brings 14.4 PTS, 5.6 REB and 3.0 AST per game, and Hachimura adds 13.1 PTS and 1.9 3PM. Hachimura is listed day-to-day, so keep an eye on him.

The model makes it **+0.094 expected wins** for the rest of season. Category gains: 3PM (+0.131), PTS (+0.076), TO (+0.024) and FT% (+0.021). The costs: REB (-0.085), BLK (-0.061), FG% (-0.026), STL (-0.023) and AST (-0.016).

Losing Diabate's 8.7 rebounds and 0.9 blocks is the real price here.

## The case for Viva La Raza!

Diabate is the prize: 8.7 REB, 1.1 STL and 0.9 BLK per game. Kuzma adds 12.1 PTS, 5.2 REB and 2.7 AST. It's a big-man, defence-flavoured package, and both players are healthy.

The model has it at **+0.033 expected wins** for the rest of season. They gain REB (+0.079), BLK (+0.058), STL (+0.038) and FG% (+0.022). They lose 3PM (-0.096), PTS (-0.058), AST (-0.035) and FT% (-0.016).

The three-point hole is big, and Kuzma's 1.7 turnovers per game won't help.

## Verdict

**JAReusalem: B+.** A clear edge in the model, and 3PM is the biggest single swing in the deal.

**Viva La Raza!: B-.** A small win on paper. They fix rebounds and blocks but give up threes and points.

Rare to see both sides come out positive, so this one is really about which categories each manager wants to chase. Now we wait for league review to wave it through.
