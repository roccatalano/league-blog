## Breaking: a 2-for-2 is in league review

**Viva La Raza!** and **mr charloooooo** have agreed a swap. It's accepted but sitting in league review, so no players have moved yet.

- **Viva La Raza! gets:** Franz Wagner and Josh Hart
- **mr charloooooo gets:** RJ Barrett and Onyeka Okongwu

## The case for Viva La Raza!

Wagner is the prize: 21.7 PTS, 6.0 REB, 5.2 AST and 1.1 STL per game. Hart adds 7.6 REB, 4.5 AST and a juicy 1.4 STL. Together they are a steals and assists machine.

The model makes it **+0.170 expected wins** for the rest of season. Category gains: STL (+0.089), AST (+0.069), FT% (+0.047) and a sliver of REB (+0.007). The costs: TO (-0.051), BLK (-0.049), FG% (-0.018), 3PM (-0.015) and PTS (-0.006).

Giving up Okongwu's 0.9 BLK stings, and Wagner's 2.7 turnovers per game won't help.

## The case for mr charloooooo

Barrett brings 19.8 PTS and 1.6 3PM, while Okongwu brings 14.0 PTS, 7.6 REB, 0.9 BLK and 1.6 3PM. It's a tidy two-man package with fewer turnovers.

The model has it at **-0.183 expected wins** for the rest of season. They gain TO (+0.072), BLK (+0.065), FG% (+0.022), 3PM (+0.015) and PTS (+0.008). They lose STL (-0.093), AST (-0.087), FT% (-0.068) and REB (-0.007).

Trading steals and dimes for blocks and fewer giveaways is a legitimate plan. The numbers just say the price is a little steep.

## Verdict

**Viva La Raza!: B+.** Two starters with real counting stats, and the steals boost is the biggest single swing in the deal.

**mr charloooooo: C+.** Not a disaster, and the turnover and block help is real. But they're giving up more than they get.

Both sides are active and healthy, so this one is all about fit. Now we wait for league review to wave it through.
