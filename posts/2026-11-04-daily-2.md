## Lead story: Popoviched take two for one

**Popoviched** are getting Dejounte Murray (19.2 PTS, 5.5 REB, 6.3 AST, 1.6 STL) and Naz Reid (16.2 PTS, 7.7 REB, 2.5 3PM) from **Torta Pounders** for Darius Acuff Jr. (19.8 PTS, 6.0 AST, 3.1 TO). The deal is accepted but still **in league review**, and the players haven't moved yet.

- **Popoviched: +0.901 expected wins** (rest of season). They gain REB, STL, BLK, PTS, 3PM and AST. They give up a little in TO and FT%. **Grade: A**
- **Torta Pounders: -0.707 expected wins.** They lose ground in 3PM, STL, PTS, REB and AST, and improve only in TO and FG%. **Grade: D**

Torta get one very good scorer who turns it over 3.1 times a game, and they pay two starters for him. Review committee, take your time.

## The other trade: processed

**JAReusalem** get Rui Hachimura and Aaron Gordon. **Viva La Raza!** get Kyle Kuzma and Moussa Diabate. It has gone through.

- **JAReusalem: +0.096.** They gain 3PM (+0.129) and PTS (+0.076) and give up REB and BLK. **Grade: B**
- **Viva La Raza!: -0.068.** They gain REB (+0.096) and BLK, but lose 3PM (-0.132) and PTS. **Grade: C+**

Small numbers either way. Diabate's 8.7 boards are real, but the shooting leaves with Gordon and Hachimura.
