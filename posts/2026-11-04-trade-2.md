Two deals, one done and one waiting on the league. Here's the damage.

## Done: forwards for a big man

**JAReusalem** gets Rui Hachimura (13.1 PTS, 1.9 3PM) and Aaron Gordon (14.4 PTS, 5.6 REB, 3.0 AST). **Viva La Raza!** gets Kyle Kuzma (12.1 PTS, 5.2 REB) and Moussa Diabate (8.7 REB, 1.1 STL, 0.9 BLK).

This trade has been processed and the players have moved.

- **JAReusalem: +0.096** expected wins, rest of season. Gains in 3PM (+0.129), PTS (+0.076), TO and FT%. Pays for it in REB (-0.083), BLK (-0.060), STL, AST and FG%.
- **Viva La Raza!: -0.068** expected wins. Gains in REB (+0.096), BLK (+0.043), STL, AST and FG%. Loses 3PM (-0.132), PTS (-0.070), TO and FT%.

Diabate is a rebounding and blocks specialist, but he shoots zero threes. JAReusalem buys scoring and range and Viva La Raza! buys boards. The model likes the scoring side a little more.

**Grades:** JAReusalem B, Viva La Raza! C+

## In review: the big one

**Popoviched** gets Dejounte Murray (19.2 PTS, 6.3 AST, 1.6 STL) and Naz Reid (16.2 PTS, 7.7 REB, 2.5 3PM). **Torta Pounders** gets Darius Acuff Jr. (19.8 PTS, 6.0 AST).

This one is accepted but sits in league review, so the players have not moved yet.

- **Popoviched: +0.901** expected wins, rest of season. Gains in REB (+0.157), STL (+0.106), BLK (+0.083), PTS, AST and 3PM. Gives up some TO (-0.052) and FT% (-0.027).
- **Torta Pounders: -0.707** expected wins. Gains only in TO (+0.048) and FG% (+0.030). Loses STL (-0.105), 3PM (-0.117), PTS, REB and AST.

That is a huge swing for a trade in which one side hands over a single player. Acuff Jr. scores a touch more than either of the incoming pair, but he comes with 3.1 turnovers a game, and Torta Pounders is giving up two rotation pieces for him. Popoviched picks up depth across seven categories. Torta Pounders gets one very good player and a thinner roster.

**Grades:** Popoviched A, Torta Pounders D
