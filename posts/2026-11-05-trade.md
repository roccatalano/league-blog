## Breaking: a 2-for-1 is in league review

A trade has been accepted and is **in league review**. The players haven't moved yet, so Torta Pounders managers, you still have a few moments to wince in advance.

**Popoviched** get Naz Reid and Dejounte Murray.
**Torta Pounders** get Darius Acuff Jr.

## The case for Popoviched

Two quality starters for one is the classic consolidation-in-reverse play. Reid brings 16.7 PTS, 7.7 REB, 1.0 BLK and 2.5 3PM per game. Murray adds 19.2 PTS, 5.6 REB, 6.2 AST and 1.7 STL. Acuff Jr. (19.9 PTS, 6.3 AST) is good, but he also turns it over 3.2 times a game, and Popoviched are swapping him for depth plus a lot of rebounds and defensive stats.

Expected win change, rest of season: **+0.946**.

Categories gained: REB (+0.165), STL (+0.110), BLK (+0.081), PTS (+0.061), 3PM (+0.027), AST (+0.024), FG% (+0.007).
Categories lost: TO (-0.052), FT% (-0.014).

**Verdict: A**

## The case for Torta Pounders

Acuff Jr. is a legitimate scorer and passer, and he's all they're getting. His 19.9 PTS and 6.3 AST are the best of the three, and trading two players for one frees a roster spot for a waiver pickup. They also improve in TO (+0.063), FT% (+0.028) and so on, as Acuff Jr. is a different kind of player.

But the rest of the ledger isn't kind. REB (-0.131), STL (-0.090), BLK (-0.064), PTS (-0.049), AST (-0.025) and FG% (-0.020) all drop.

Expected win change, rest of season: **-0.707**.

**Verdict: D+**

## Bottom line

The model (weeks 3-18) sees this as a big swing toward Popoviched. Torta Pounders will need to turn that open roster spot into something useful on the wire. If the league lets it through, we'll see who was right. 🏀
