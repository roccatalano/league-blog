## Breaking: a one-for-one big man swap

A trade has been accepted and is **in league review**. The players haven't moved yet, so nothing is final until the review clears.

**Torta Pounders** get Paul Reed. **Viva La Raza!** get Neemias Queta.

## The case for each side

**Viva La Raza!** are buying rebounds and blocks. Queta's model line is 9.6 PTS, 7.2 REB, 1.5 AST, 0.5 STL, 1.3 BLK and 0.9 TO per game. Reed brings 9.3 PTS, 5.2 REB and 1.0 STL, so this team gives up a little in steals and gets a lot more on the glass and at the rim.

**Torta Pounders** are chasing a different shape of big. Reed gives them 1.0 STL and 0.3 3PM per game, but he costs them 2.0 rebounds and 0.4 blocks a night. He also turns it over a bit more, at 1.2 TO against 0.9.

## Verdict

**Viva La Raza!: B+.** They get the better rebounder and shot-blocker and the higher expected win change. The catch is a 3PM hit of -0.123, the biggest single swing in the deal. If they were punting threes already, this looks even better.

**Torta Pounders: C.** The steals bump is real, but it doesn't make up for the lost rebounds and blocks. The model has the trade costing them 0.076 wins over the rest of the season, so they'll need Reed to outperform his projection to come out ahead.

Both players are listed as active, so there's no injury angle. We'll see whether league review lets it through.
