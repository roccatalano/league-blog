## The deal

- **Torta Pounders** get Paul Reed
- **Viva La Raza!** get Neemias Queta

Both players are healthy, and both are projected at 9.3 points a game. The differences are in the details.

## The case for Torta Pounders

Reed brings more steals (1.0 vs 0.5), more assists (1.6 vs 1.4) and a few more threes (0.3 vs 0.1). The cost is rebounds (5.2 vs 7.0) and blocks (0.9 vs 1.3), and he turns the ball over more (1.2 vs 0.9).

Torta Pounders gain in steals (+0.051), 3PM (+0.012), assists (+0.006) and FT% (+0.019). They lose in rebounds (-0.038), blocks (-0.049) and turnovers (-0.016).

Expected win change, rest of season: **-0.056**

Verdict: they trade rebounds and blocks for steals and a little scattered help. The model doesn't love the math.

**Grade: C-**

## The case for Viva La Raza!

Queta is the better board-cleaner and rim protector, and he turns it over less. Viva La Raza! gain in blocks (+0.109), rebounds (+0.075), FG% (+0.051) and turnovers (+0.012). They give up 3PM (-0.123), assists (-0.059), steals (-0.026) and FT% (-0.023).

The 3PM drop is the biggest single number on either side of this trade, so a team that leans on threes from this slot will feel it. Still, the blocks and rebounds more than cover it.

Expected win change, rest of season: **+0.040**

Verdict: a small but real win by picking the category lanes where Queta moves the needle.

**Grade: B**

## Bottom line

Nobody's season is made or broken here. Viva La Raza! take a modest edge, and Torta Pounders pay a small tax to get their steals. Reed and Queta will each be watched closely over the coming weeks to see whether the model's call holds up.
