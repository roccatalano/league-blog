## Injury watch

A lot of Denver Nuggets are questionable for Monday's game against Houston, and it hits plenty of rosters:

- **The Serbian Empire** have Nikola Jokic (elbow) questionable, plus Immanuel Quickley (hamstring) and Quentin Grimes (finger), with Brandon Miller (groin) out and projected back around 12 November.
- **The Real OG** have Cameron Johnson questionable, and the longer-term losses are heavy: Paolo Banchero (high-ankle sprain, four-to-six weeks, projected back around 22 November) and Kon Knueppel (hamstring strain, re-evaluated in four weeks, projected back around 12 December).
- **mr charloooooo** are without Jaren Jackson Jr. (torn MCL, six-to-eight weeks, projected back around 14 December) and Ja Morant (hamstring, projected back around 1 December), though Morant's coach said he "may not be out for as long as we thought".
- **The haliban** have Anthony Edwards out after a knee procedure, projected back around 21 December, and Dennis Schroder (hamstring strain) out about two more weeks.
- **Stairway to Devin** lost Chet Holmgren to a patellar tendon tear, with at least 8-10 weeks to go.
- **Al-Shaida** have Jimmy Butler III working back from a torn ACL, with a return between January and February, and Luka Doncic is day-to-day with back tightness.

Projected dates are projections, not promises.
