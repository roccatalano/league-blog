## Breaking: a one-for-one blockbuster 🚨

**Stairway to Devin** and **The Serbian Empire** have agreed a swap. The deal is accepted but still in league review, so the players haven't moved yet.

- **Stairway to Devin gets:** Immanuel Quickley (currently day-to-day)
- **The Serbian Empire gets:** Dylan Harper (active)

## The case for Stairway to Devin

Quickley brings 17.2 PTS, 3.8 REB, 5.8 AST, 1.1 STL and 2.6 3PM per game, with only 1.3 TO. Harper offers 16.3 PTS, 4.8 REB, 4.7 AST, 0.9 STL, 1.2 3PM and 1.8 TO.

Quickley wins on points, assists, steals, turnovers and especially threes, where he has 1.4 more per game. The model projects **+0.126 expected wins** for the rest of the season.

Categories gained: 3PM (+0.066), AST (+0.024), STL (+0.019), TO (+0.018) and PTS (+0.007). Categories lost: FG% (-0.028), BLK (-0.026) and REB (-0.025).

**Verdict: B+.** It's a clear upgrade, though the day-to-day tag is a small worry.

## The case for The Serbian Empire

Harper is healthy and gives more rebounds and a touch more blocks. He's also younger, if you're thinking long term. But the model projects **-0.170 expected wins** for the rest of the season, the bigger swing of the two.

Categories gained: FG% (+0.028), BLK (+0.030) and REB (+0.024). Categories lost: 3PM (-0.079), AST (-0.025), TO (-0.024), STL (-0.019) and PTS (-0.007).

Giving up that many threes hurts, and the three small gains don't cover it.

**Verdict: C.** The Serbian Empire gets the healthier player but loses a good chunk of win equity.

## Bottom line

The numbers cover weeks 4-18 and say Stairway to Devin comes out ahead. Watch for the review to clear and for Quickley's status to update.
