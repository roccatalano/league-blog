Two deals, two very different vibes. One is done. The other is still in the oven.

## Done: Quickley for Harper (processed)

**Stairway to Devin** gets Immanuel Quickley (17.2 PTS, 5.8 AST, 2.6 3PM, listed day-to-day). **The Serbian Empire** gets Dylan Harper (16.3 PTS, 4.8 REB, 4.7 AST, active).

**Stairway to Devin: +0.133 expected wins, rest of season.** Quickley brings the threes, assists and steals, and fewer turnovers. The 3PM win rate jumps +0.066. The price is rebounds (-0.025), blocks (-0.025) and FG% (-0.030). Grade: **B**. The day-to-day tag is a small asterisk.

**The Serbian Empire: -0.176 expected wins.** Harper helps in FG% (+0.031), blocks (+0.028) and rebounds (+0.023), but losing Quickley's threes costs -0.079 in 3PM. That's a lot to give up for a rebound bump. Grade: **C-**.

## In review: the four-player swap

Still in league review, and the players haven't moved yet.

**Derrick White Girl Wasted** gets Collin Gillespie and Rudy Gobert. **Gets On Base** gets Jared McCain and Darius Garland.

**Derrick White Girl Wasted: -0.021 expected wins.** Gobert (11.9 REB, 1.4 BLK) is a category cheat code: rebounds +0.217, blocks +0.130, turnovers +0.121. But points (-0.148), threes (-0.175) and FT% (-0.130) take a beating. Grade: **C**. It's a fun build, but the numbers say it's basically a wash.

**Gets On Base: +0.083 expected wins.** Garland (21.5 PTS, 7.3 AST, 2.8 3PM) and McCain add points (+0.155), threes (+0.180), FT% (+0.098) and assists (+0.081). The bill is rebounds (-0.176), blocks (-0.118) and turnovers (-0.143), since Garland averages 3.9 TO. Grade: **B**.

Verdict: if it clears review, Gets On Base is the one smiling. Gobert's board work is real, but giving up this much scoring and shooting costs more than it returns.

All expected-win figures cover the rest of the season (weeks 4-18).
