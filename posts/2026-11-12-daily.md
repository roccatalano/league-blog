## Trade review: a win-win, according to the model

**Derrick White Girl Wasted** get Collin Gillespie and Rudy Gobert. **Gets On Base** get Jared McCain and Darius Garland. The trade has been processed.

**Derrick White Girl Wasted: +0.047 expected wins (rest of season), Grade B.** Gobert brings 11.9 boards and 1.4 blocks a game, and the model has the trade lifting their REB (+0.222) and BLK (+0.133) category win rates. The cost is points (-0.142), 3PM (-0.177) and FT% (-0.138). Their turnovers improve (+0.126) with Garland's 3.9 a night gone.

**Gets On Base: +0.040 expected wins, Grade B-.** Garland (21.1 PTS, 7.2 AST) and McCain (2.6 3PM) bring scoring (+0.152), threes (+0.176) and free throws (+0.100). They give up rebounds (-0.174), blocks (-0.122) and turnovers (-0.146).

Verdict: both sides fixed a weakness by selling a strength, and both come out slightly ahead. Rare, and kind of boring. We expected a heist.
