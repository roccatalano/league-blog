A two-for-two just landed, and **OnlyFranz** finally owns a Franz. It's accepted but still in league review, so the players haven't moved yet.

## The deal

- **OnlyFranz** gets: Franz Wagner and Josh Hart
- **Viva La Raza!** gets: Amen Thompson and Ayo Dosunmu

## The case for OnlyFranz

Wagner is the prize: 22.9 points, 6.1 rebounds, 5.8 assists, 1.3 steals and 1.4 threes per game in the model. Hart adds 7.4 boards, 4.6 assists and 1.4 steals. OnlyFranz gains the most in assists (+0.071), threes (+0.046) and rebounds (+0.043).

The costs are blocks (-0.064), turnovers (-0.063) and FG% (-0.044). Wagner's 3.1 turnovers a night won't help the last two.

Expected win change, rest of season: **+0.055**.

## The case for Viva La Raza!

Thompson (16.6 points, 7.3 rebounds, 4.2 assists, 1.4 steals, 0.7 blocks) and Dosunmu (16.5 points, 1.9 threes, 1.1 steals) are two solid, balanced players. **Viva La Raza!** picks up blocks (+0.064), turnovers (+0.063) and FG% (+0.047) as the cleaner, lower-mistake pair.

They pay for it in assists (-0.068), rebounds (-0.051) and threes (-0.039). Giving up their top scorer is also why points dip slightly (-0.007).

Expected win change, rest of season: **-0.039**.

## Verdict

OnlyFranz wins on the numbers, but only just. The model counts weeks 4-18, and these gains are small. Viva La Raza! isn't being fleeced. They're trading volume and assists for tidier categories, and that could pay off in a close week.

- **OnlyFranz: B+**
- **Viva La Raza!: C+**

We'll see whether league review lets it through. 🏀
