## Injury watch: the infirmary is busy

**Al-Shaida** had a rough night. Luka Doncic dropped 32 points, five boards, five assists and three steals, then limped to the locker room late with an apparent right ankle injury. Joel Embiid (foot) and Austin Reaves (left heel) are also day-to-day. Reaves' X-rays were negative, but he gets more imaging Thursday and is projected back around 15 November.

**mr charloooooo** has a bigger worry. Steve Kerr said the concern level on Stephen Curry's abdominal tweak is "high" while they await MRI results. Jaren Jackson Jr. (torn MCL) is projected back 14 December, and Ja Morant is projected back 1 December, though his coach says he "may not be out for as long as we thought".

Elsewhere, **The Serbian Empire** lost Brandon Miller (groin, out, projected back around 15 November). **Torta Pounders** are without Lauri Markkanen (fractured hand, at least a month) on top of Tyrese Haliburton and Jusuf Nurkic. **The haliban** are missing Anthony Edwards until about 21 December.
