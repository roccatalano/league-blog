## Trade alert 🚨

A two-for-two has been accepted and is **in league review**. The players haven't moved yet, so nobody's lineup has changed.

**OnlyFranz** gets Franz Wagner and Josh Hart. **Viva La Raza!** gets Ayo Dosunmu and Amen Thompson.

## The case for OnlyFranz

Wagner is the prize: 22.9 points, 6.1 rebounds and 5.8 assists per game in the model. Hart adds 7.4 boards, 4.6 assists, 1.4 steals and 1.5 threes on top. Between them, that's a lot of box-score filler.

The cost is on the defensive and efficiency side. Rest-of-season category win rate drops in blocks (-0.066), turnovers (-0.063) and FG% (-0.045). Wagner's 3.1 turnovers a night won't help that last one. In return they gain assists (+0.074), threes (+0.047), rebounds (+0.045), steals (+0.025) and points (+0.007).

**Expected wins, rest of season: +0.061**

**Grade: B**

## The case for Viva La Raza!

Dosunmu (16.5 points, 1.9 threes) and Thompson (16.6 points, 7.3 rebounds, 1.4 steals, 0.7 blocks) are two solid, balanced players. They bring blocks (+0.067), turnovers (+0.064) and FG% (+0.047) up, which is exactly the area the other side is giving away.

But they pay for it in assists (-0.070), rebounds (-0.053), threes (-0.040), steals (-0.030) and points (-0.007). Giving up a 22.9-point scorer is a real hit.

**Expected wins, rest of season: -0.038**

**Grade: C+**

## Verdict

The model says OnlyFranz wins this one, but not by a mile. Both sides swap four healthy players (all listed ACTIVE) and both fix one weakness while opening another. It's a classic trade of volume for efficiency. The numbers use weeks 4-18.

Keep an eye on the league review. If it clears, expect the lineups to look very different by the next matchup.
