## The deal

**Stairway to Devin** and **Viva La Raza!** have agreed a two-for-two. It has been accepted but is **still in league review**, so no players have moved yet.

- **Stairway to Devin gets:** AJ Dybantsa and Neemias Queta
- **Viva La Raza! gets:** Matas Buzelis and Meleek Thomas

## Stairway to Devin: the big-man bet

Dybantsa is the headline: 18.2 PTS, 5.4 REB, 2.6 AST and 1.0 STL per game in the model. Queta adds 7.3 REB and 1.3 BLK. Stairway are buying points, steals and a real rebounding base.

The cost is threes. Buzelis (2.1 3PM) and Thomas (1.6 3PM) leave, and the 3PM win rate against the league drops 0.056. Blocks fall 0.060 too, despite Queta, because Buzelis blocks 1.2 a game.

- **Gains:** PTS (+0.024), STL (+0.045), AST (+0.013), TO (+0.011)
- **Loses:** BLK, 3PM, FT% (-0.017)
- **Expected wins, rest of season: -0.096**

**Verdict: C+.** The star power is real, but the category math doesn't back it.

## Viva La Raza!: the three-point play

Viva get two shooters who fit one clear need. The 3PM win rate jumps 0.162, the biggest single move in the deal. They also gain on AST (+0.055) and FT% (+0.054).

The price is the paint. Losing Queta costs 0.110 in REB, and blocks drop 0.064. PTS (-0.012), TO (-0.037) and FG% (-0.043) slip as well.

- **Gains:** 3PM, AST, FT%
- **Loses:** REB, BLK, FG%, TO, PTS
- **Expected wins, rest of season: -0.005**

**Verdict: B-.** Almost a wash, and the best of a mildly negative pair.

## The takeaway

Both teams show a negative expected win change, which is unusual for a trade. Stairway lose about a tenth of a win over the rest of the season (weeks 4-18 counted). Viva basically break even. Neither side is getting fleeced, but neither is clearly winning.

Watch for the league review result. Until it clears, everyone stays where they are. 🏀
