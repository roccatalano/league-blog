Two deals hit the wire. One is done, one is still waiting on the league's review committee.

## Done: Stairway to Devin and Viva La Raza!

**Stairway to Devin** gets Neemias Queta (9.3 PTS, 7.2 REB, 1.2 BLK per game) and AJ Dybantsa (18.1 PTS, 5.4 REB, 2.6 AST). **Viva La Raza!** gets Matas Buzelis (15.3 PTS, 2.1 3PM) and Meleek Thomas (10.4 PTS, 1.6 3PM). This one has been processed.

Stairway buys points, steals and assists. They give up blocks and threes. Their category win rate vs the league moves +0.045 in STL and +0.023 in PTS, but falls -0.064 in BLK and -0.057 in 3PM. Expected wins for the rest of the season: **-0.111**.

Viva La Raza! gets the long-range help, with 3PM up +0.086 and BLK up +0.061. They lose ground in STL (-0.044) and PTS (-0.028). Expected wins: **+0.132**.

**Verdict:** Viva takes it. Dybantsa is the prize, but two guys who shoot threes and block shots fit Viva's gaps better. Stairway to Devin: **C+**. Viva La Raza!: **B+**.

## In review: OnlyFranz and The haliban

This one is accepted but sits in league review. The players haven't moved yet.

**OnlyFranz** gets John Collins and Miles Bridges (17.1 PTS, 6.8 REB, 3.3 AST). **The haliban** gets Rui Hachimura, Aaron Gordon and Josh Hart (10.0 PTS, 7.2 REB, 4.4 AST, 1.4 STL).

OnlyFranz is turning three players into two, and the model loves it. BLK jumps +0.221 and REB +0.072. They give up AST (-0.097) and STL (-0.055). Expected wins: **+0.464**. That is the biggest swing in either deal.

The haliban gets depth and assists (AST +0.075, STL +0.059) but loses BLK (-0.090) and REB (-0.056). Expected wins: **-0.216**. Quantity over quality is a classic fantasy trap.

**Verdict:** OnlyFranz wins this clearly. OnlyFranz: **A-**. The haliban: **D+**.

All numbers cover weeks 5-18. If the review committee blocks the second deal, expect some noise in the comments.
