Week 4 is in the books. Four weeks down, 14 to go, and the top six make the finals. Two teams are still perfect, six are stuck at 1-3, and one of them just got hit with a ten-point power ranking drop. Let's get into it.

**Giddey up Horsey** went 7-2-0 over **The haliban**, the biggest margin of the week. Horsey took PTS (654 to 394), REB, AST, STL, 3PM, FG% and FT%. The haliban's only wins were BLK and TO, which is a strange way to lose by five.

**HE IS HIM ( G.O.A.T)** beat **Derrick White Girl Wasted** 6-3-0. **OnlyFranz** beat **Torta Pounders** 6-3-0 and stays perfect at 4-0.

**Viva La Raza!** edged **Popoviched** 5-3-1. STL finished 40-40, the only tie of the week.

Three matchups were decided by a single category:

- **Gets On Base** beat **The Real OG** 5-4-0
- **Al-Shaida** beat **mr charloooooo** 5-4-0

## Awards

**Upset of the week:** Gets On Base over The Real OG at an opening price of $8.00.

**Comeback of the week:** the same game. Gets On Base started at a 4% win chance.

**Choke of the week:** The Real OG, who hit 96% at the opening and still lost 5-4. They won PTS (783 to 655), AST, STL and FT%, but TO and FG% sank them. Gets On Base's bench produced 92.1, the most of any team, and Norman Powell's 33-point bench game (44.5) was the biggest single one.

**Bad beat:** Al-Shaida lost FT% to mr charloooooo by .785 to .787. They still won the matchup, so they'll live.

**Team of the week:** Giddey up Horsey, with their 7-2-0 win. They still sit at 1-3, and their luck rating of -1.5 is the worst in the league.

**MVP of the week:** **Victor Wembanyama** of HE IS HIM ( G.O.A.T): 110 pts, 42 reb, 16 ast, 4 stl, 20 blk and 8 3pm over four games, on 43/69 shooting. Twenty blocks in a week is absurd.

**Best acquisition:** **Jakob Poeltl** (Viva La Raza!, $12 bid): 44 pts, 26 reb, 5 blk in four games, or 25.2 per game.

**Worst acquisition:** **Kris Dunn** (mr charloooooo, $15 bid): 12 pts, 8 reb, 10 ast in three games, 12.0 per game. Not a disaster, but not $15.

**Worst drop:** **Meleek Thomas**, dropped by Derrick White Girl Wasted and now on Viva La Raza!. He's averaging 16.0 pts and 2.8 3pm over his last four games.

**Streaks:** Al-Shaida's W4 is the longest winning streak, and OnlyFranz also have four wins. Popoviched's L3 is the longest losing one.

**Luckiest team:** OnlyFranz, with 4 wins against 2.5 expected (+1.5). Al-Shaida are next at +1.4.
