## The case for Viva La Raza!

LaMelo is a three-point machine at 3.9 a game, and Naz chips in 7.9 boards, 2.5 threes and 0.9 blocks. Two active players, no injury tag, and a lot of counting stats.

Expected win change, rest of season: **-0.046**.

Gains: 3PM (+0.175), BLK (+0.025) and REB (+0.022).
Loses: AST (-0.119), PTS (-0.023), FG% (-0.029), FT% (-0.030), TO (-0.027) and STL (-0.017).

**Verdict: C+.** The threes boost is real, but giving up the assists and the efficiency categories costs more than it brings.

## Bottom line

This is a category swap more than a talent swap. Popoviched is paying in threes for assists, and the model says that trade favours them. Viva La Raza! now has a 3PM edge and a hole at assists. Numbers are based on weeks 5-18 of projections, so your mileage may vary. 🏀
