Two deals, two very different verdicts. All numbers are rest-of-season expected wins.

## Trade 1: Popoviched gets the stars (in league review)

**Popoviched** gets Trae Young and Bam Adebayo. **Viva La Raza!** gets Naz Reid and LaMelo Ball. It's accepted but still in league review, so nobody has moved yet.

Popoviched is buying assists and steals: Trae brings 9.9 AST per game, and Bam adds 9.0 REB and 1.6 STL (he's day-to-day, so keep an eye on him). The model says **+0.128** wins, with AST (+0.115) the big gainer. The price is 3PM (-0.128), since LaMelo's 3.9 threes per game walk out the door.

Viva La Raza! pockets that 3PM boost (+0.171) and a little REB and BLK, but gives up AST (-0.115) and PTS. Net: **-0.071**.

- **Popoviched: B+**
- **Viva La Raza!: C**

## Trade 2: OnlyFranz robs The haliban (processed)

This one is done. **OnlyFranz** sends Rui Hachimura, Josh Hart and Aaron Gordon to **The haliban** for Miles Bridges and John Collins.

Franz turns three mid-range bodies into two better ones: Bridges (17.1 PTS, 6.8 REB) and Collins (13.7 PTS, 6.9 REB, 0.9 BLK). The big win is blocks, up **+0.225** in category win rate, with rebounds (+0.076) also rising. Franz gives up some AST (-0.096) and STL (-0.053). Total: **+0.479** wins, a monster number for one trade.

The haliban gets volume and depth, with Hart's 4.4 AST and 1.4 STL doing the heavy lifting. But BLK drops by 0.100 and PTS slips, and the model puts the damage at **-0.192** wins. Three players for two sounds like a haul. The numbers disagree.

- **OnlyFranz: A**
- **The haliban: D+**

## Bottom line

Franz made the best deal of the week. Popoviched has a real, if smaller, edge, and the Viva La Raza! side hinges on whether the threes are worth the assists. Managers who watch the 3PM column should take note. 📈
