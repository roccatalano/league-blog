## The big story: a 7-2 lead nobody believes in

**Gets On Base** leads **Popoviched** 7-2 in categories, and the model still makes Popoviched the favourite at 63% ($1.49) against 37% ($2.35) for Gets On Base. Seven categories up and the underdog? Somebody's holding a lot of volume in the wrong places. Popoviched have drifted from $1.20 to $1.49 since the last post, so the market is wobbling, but it still isn't buying the scoreboard.

Meanwhile, **HE IS HIM ( G.O.A.T)** also lead 7-2, over **mr charloooooo**, and here the model agrees: 100% ($1.01). Charlo is the $9.00 longshot. The only hope is FG%, where it's .472 to .468. That's the single closest category in the matchup.

## Injury watch

The ugly part of the report is the long-term list:

- **The haliban** are missing Anthony Edwards, who had an arthroscopic knee procedure and is projected back around 21 December (12 games missed so far). Tobias Harris (calf, projected early December) and Dereck Lively II (foot, projected 27 November) are out as well. That's a lot of firepower on the shelf for the team that just firmed to $2.15.
- **mr charloooooo** are stacked with injuries: Jaren Jackson Jr. has a torn MCL and a 6-to-8 week timetable, Stephen Curry has an oblique abdominal tear (re-evaluated in two weeks), Evan Mobley has a toe sprain, and Ja Morant is still out. RJ Barrett is now day-to-day with a hamstring too.
- **Al-Shaida** lost Austin Reaves, who is out with a heel injury and has no official timetable. Scottie Barnes is day-to-day, and Jimmy Butler III (torn ACL) is projected back in early January at best.
- **The Serbian Empire** will be without Dylan Harper (hip) for Thursday's game against the Clippers.
- **Stairway to Devin** have Chet Holmgren out at least 8-10 weeks. Projected back around 2 January.
- **Torta Pounders** lost Lauri Markkanen to a hand fracture, at least a month out.
- **The Real OG** have Paolo Banchero (four-to-six weeks) and Kon Knueppel (re-evaluated in four weeks) both on the shelf.

Return dates are projections, so treat them as rough guides.
