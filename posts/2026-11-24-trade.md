Trade alert. A 2-for-2 just landed, and it is **accepted but still in league review**, so no players have moved yet.

## The deal

**Al-Shaida** gets: Tyrese Haliburton and Malik Monk.
**Torta Pounders** gets: Kevin Durant and Kelly Oubre Jr.

## Al-Shaida: the assist merchant's pivot

Haliburton is currently listed OUT, which is a bold thing to build a trade around. When he's back, he's the prize: 19.7 points, 9.8 assists, 1.3 steals and 2.8 threes per game in the model. Monk (ACTIVE) adds 11.3 points, 4.0 assists and 1.6 threes as the plug-in piece.

Al-Shaida loses scoring and rebounding, but takes a huge swing in assists.

- **Expected win change, rest of season: +0.108**
- Gains: AST (+0.130), STL (+0.059), 3PM (+0.018), TO (+0.016)
- Loses: PTS (-0.063), REB (-0.043), BLK (-0.020), FG% (-0.018), FT% (-0.014)

**Verdict:** Patience pays if the injury is short. Durant's 2.9 turnovers a game walking out the door helps too. **Grade: B**

## Torta Pounders: buying points now

Durant (ACTIVE) brings 23.1 points, 5.5 rebounds, 4.1 assists and 1.0 blocks. Oubre (ACTIVE) adds 12.3 points, 3.9 rebounds and 0.8 steals. Both are healthy, which is a nice change from what they're sending out.

The catch: Haliburton's assists are very hard to replace, and that category takes the hit.

- **Expected win change, rest of season: -0.093**
- Gains: BLK (+0.080), REB (+0.068), PTS (+0.058), FG% (+0.039), FT% (+0.010)
- Loses: AST (-0.225), 3PM (-0.058), STL (-0.035), TO (-0.013)

**Verdict:** Real firepower, but giving up the assist engine costs more than the points bring back. **Grade: C+**

## Bottom line

By the model, Al-Shaida wins this one, though Torta gets the two players who can actually play tonight. The numbers cover weeks 6-18. We'll see if the league lets it through. 🏀
