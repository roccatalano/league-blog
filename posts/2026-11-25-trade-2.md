## The deal

It's done. This one has been **processed**, so no league review drama.

**Al-Shaida** gets Tyrese Haliburton (currently OUT) and Malik Monk.

**Torta Pounders** get Kevin Durant and Kelly Oubre Jr.

## The case for Al-Shaida

Haliburton is the prize here: 19.7 points, 9.8 assists, 1.3 steals and 2.8 threes per game in the model. Monk adds 11.3 points, 4.0 assists and 1.6 threes. The catch is that Haliburton is listed as OUT, so Al-Shaida are paying full price for a player who isn't playing right now. That's patience, or optimism.

The assist category is where this pays off: **+0.125** in category win rate, plus +0.060 in steals. Points (-0.064) and rebounds (-0.042) take a hit, and Durant's 2.9 turnovers per game leaving is a small help.

Expected win change, rest of season: **+0.107**.

**Verdict: B+.** It's a good deal on paper, as long as Haliburton gets back on the court.

## The case for Torta Pounders

Durant is active and puts up 23.1 points, 5.5 rebounds, 4.1 assists and 1.0 blocks per game. Oubre chips in 12.3 points, 3.9 boards and 0.8 steals. Torta gain in blocks (+0.070), points (+0.060), rebounds (+0.049) and field goal percentage (+0.040), with a small gain in free throw percentage (+0.010).

The price is assists. That category win rate falls **-0.199**, the biggest swing in the trade. Threes (-0.051) and steals (-0.032) also slip.

Expected win change, rest of season: **-0.081**.

**Verdict: C+.** Torta get healthy bodies and a star scorer, but giving up Haliburton's assists costs more than Durant's scoring brings back. They'll need to find assists elsewhere.

## Bottom line

Al-Shaida win the numbers, Torta win the "I can play him tonight" argument. The model says the sellers of the injured star come out behind, though only if Haliburton returns. 🏀
