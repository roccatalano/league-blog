Two deals are on the board. Both are accepted but **still in league review**, so no players have moved yet.

## Blockbuster: quantity vs. size

**Gets On Base** gets LaMelo Ball (day-to-day), Ayo Dosunmu and Andrew Wiggins.
**Viva La Raza!** gets Ace Bailey, Jared McCain and Donovan Clingan.

The case for Gets On Base: Ball alone brings 20.2 PTS, 6.9 AST, 1.5 STL and 3.8 3PM per game. Dosunmu adds 16.8 PTS and Wiggins adds depth. They gain PTS (+0.098), AST (+0.188), STL (+0.105) and 3PM (+0.134). They pay in BLK (-0.180), REB (-0.163) and TO (-0.111).

The case for Viva La Raza!: Clingan is an 11.0 REB, 1.9 BLK anchor. That lifts REB (+0.179) and BLK (+0.175), and TO improves (+0.111). They lose AST (-0.197), 3PM (-0.130), PTS (-0.103), STL (-0.106) and FT% (-0.061).

**Expected wins, rest of season:**
- Gets On Base: **+0.189**
- Viva La Raza!: **-0.226**

**Verdict:** Gets On Base wins on volume, and Viva La Raza! hands over its guard depth for one big. Ball's day-to-day tag is the only wrinkle.
Grades: Gets On Base **B+**, Viva La Raza! **C**.

## Guard swap: White for Nembhard

**Derrick White Girl Wasted** gets Derrick White (14.3 PTS, 5.1 AST, 0.9 BLK, 2.3 3PM). **The haliban** gets Andrew Nembhard (14.3 PTS, 6.7 AST, 2.3 TO).

The scoring is identical. The difference is that White brings more blocks, steals, threes and fewer turnovers, while Nembhard brings more assists.

**Expected wins, rest of season:**
- Derrick White Girl Wasted: **+0.303**, with BLK +0.092 the biggest gain
- The haliban: **-0.241**, losing BLK (-0.075) and gaining AST (+0.042)

**Verdict:** White Girl Wasted gets the better fit. The haliban gets some assists and a few extra turnovers.
Grades: Derrick White Girl Wasted **A-**, The haliban **D+**.

We'll see whether league review lets both through. 🏀
