Another three-for-three, and this one has **gone through** (status: processed).

## The deal

**Gets On Base** receives Ayo Dosunmu, LaMelo Ball and Andrew Wiggins.

**Viva La Raza!** receives Jared McCain, Ace Bailey and Donovan Clingan.

## The case for Gets On Base

LaMelo is the prize: 20.2 points, 6.9 assists, 1.5 steals and 3.8 threes per game in the model. Dosunmu adds 16.8 points and 3.7 assists, and Wiggins chips in 12.2 points with 0.9 blocks. The catch is that Ball and Wiggins are both listed day-to-day, and LaMelo brings 3.3 turnovers a game with him.

Gets On Base's rest-of-season expected win change is **+0.176**. It gains in assists (+0.191), threes (+0.136), steals (+0.104), points (+0.095) and FT% (+0.048). It pays in blocks (-0.184), rebounds (-0.169), turnovers (-0.114) and FG% (-0.021).

**Verdict:** a guard-heavy bet that wins more than it loses. **Grade: B+**

## The case for Viva La Raza!

Clingan is the engine here, with 11.1 rebounds and 1.9 blocks a game. Bailey adds 5.3 boards and 0.9 blocks, and McCain brings 2.6 threes. All three are healthy, which is more than the other side can say. They also turn the ball over less.

Viva La Raza!'s expected win change is **-0.216**. It gains in rebounds (+0.183), blocks (+0.179), turnovers (+0.114) and FG% (+0.015). It gives up assists (-0.200), points (-0.100), threes (-0.132), steals (-0.104) and FT% (-0.063).

Trading away three starters-worth of production for a rebounding and blocks boost leaves it behind on the model's numbers.

**Verdict:** a clean specialist build, but the model says it costs wins. **Grade: C+**

## Bottom line

Gets On Base wins on the model's numbers, and Viva La Raza! is the one that has to prove the model wrong. If LaMelo and Wiggins stay healthy, Gets On Base looks even better. 🏀
