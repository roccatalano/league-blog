A three-for-three blockbuster just landed. It has been accepted but is **still in league review**, so no players have moved yet.

## The deal

**Popoviched** gets Ja Morant, Evan Mobley and RJ Barrett.

**mr charloooooo** gets Alex Sarr, Duncan Robinson and Jalen Smith.

Notice anything about the incoming trio for Popoviched? All three are listed as OUT. That's a lot of patience to buy in one transaction.

## The case for Popoviched

Raw talent. Per game, the model has Morant at 20.4 PTS / 7.0 AST, Mobley at 18.0 PTS / 8.7 REB / 1.4 BLK, and Barrett at 20.3 PTS / 6.0 REB / 4.1 AST. That's a pile of scoring and assists for a team that gives up a lot of depth.

Expected win change, rest of season: **+0.113**.

Category gains: AST (+0.240), PTS (+0.185) and FG% (+0.021), plus a sliver in STL (+0.011). The price: BLK (-0.142), TO (-0.181), FT% (-0.056), REB (-0.023) and 3PM (-0.008). Morant's 3.0 turnovers a night won't help.

**Verdict: B.** Big upside, but you can't start anyone on the injury list.

## The case for mr charloooooo

Healthier bodies and cleaner stats. Sarr (13.5 PTS / 7.0 REB / 1.6 BLK) is active, Smith is day-to-day, and Robinson brings 3.3 threes a game, though he's currently OUT too. This side wins the categories that quietly decide matchups: TO (+0.167), BLK (+0.075), 3PM (+0.084) and FT% (+0.071).

The cost is real. PTS (-0.178) and AST (-0.214) take a hit, along with REB (-0.033), STL (-0.015) and FG% (-0.014).

Expected win change, rest of season: **-0.105**.

**Verdict: C+.** Sensible depth, but giving up three high-usage players leaves a mark on the scoring column.

## Bottom line

The model likes Popoviched by a small margin (figures use weeks 6-18). If the injured trio gets healthy, it could look better still. Watch the review window. 🏀
