## The case for Popoviched

Pure star power. Morant brings 20.4 PTS and 7.0 AST per game, Barrett adds 20.3 PTS, and Mobley chips in 18.0 PTS, 8.7 REB and 1.4 BLK. The catch: all three are currently listed as OUT. That's a lot of faith to put in the injury report.

- **Expected wins, rest of season: +0.102**

- **Loses:** TO (-0.181, Morant alone averages 3.0 a night), BLK (-0.141), FT% (-0.057), REB (-0.023), 3PM (-0.009)

**Verdict: B+.** Big upside, but they're paying for it in turnovers and blocks, and they need those three healthy.

## The case for mr charloooooo

Quantity over quality. Robinson drains 3.3 threes a game, Sarr gives 7.0 REB and 1.6 BLK, and Smith adds 7.0 REB and 1.7 3PM. Importantly, Sarr is ACTIVE and Smith is day-to-day, while Robinson is OUT. Charlo also sheds Morant's turnovers, which is the biggest category win here.

- **Expected wins, rest of season: -0.075**
- **Gains:** TO (+0.181), 3PM (+0.093), BLK (+0.072), FT% (+0.070)
- **Loses:** AST (-0.228), PTS (-0.173), REB (-0.035), STL (-0.012), FG% (-0.012)

**Verdict: C+.** A tidy structural fix for turnovers and threes, but giving up that much scoring and assists hurts. Healthier bodies are the selling point.

## Bottom line

Popoviched win the model's vote at +0.102 to -0.075, though it's a modest edge rather than a heist. If the injured trio come back strong, this looks lopsided. If not, charlo's depth play looks smart. We'll check back. 🍿
